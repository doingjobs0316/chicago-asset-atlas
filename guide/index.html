For everyone

The map

Open chicagoassetatlas.org. Nothing to install, no account needed.

Look around

  • Switches turn each kind of place on or off: Peace Fellows, Other resources, Gems, Schools, Parks and Ward offices.
  • Neighborhood zooms to one community area.
  • Gem types show only the gems you pick.
  • List shows everything as cards. Español switches the language.

Open a place

  • Click a pin to see its card: what it does, address, website, and who leads it.
  • Double-click a neighborhood to open it in 3D with its buildings. The box at the bottom counts what’s there.
  • Share on a card copies a link straight to that place.

What the pins mean

The ⓘ button at the end of the switches shows this key on the map too.

  • Peace Fellows organization, 2019 to 2025 cohorts
  • Peace Fellows organization, 2026 cohort
  • Other resource: a community organization or service
  • Gem: a public place; the color and icon show its type (food, art, outdoors…)
  • Red outline: just added, not reviewed yet

Watch the network grow

Timeline plays the Peace Fellows organizations appearing cohort by cohort, from 2019 to 2026. Drag the slider to any year. Back to filters closes it.

On a phone

The filters fold away behind a Filters button over the map, so the map has more room. Tap it to change what shows, then tap Done.

For everyone

Adding a place

Click Add to the map at the top of the site. A new place shows on the map right away with a red outline, until the Goldin team checks it.

Choose what to add

  • An organization. The form asks “Are you a Goldin fellow?”. Yes: pick your cohort year. No: pick what kind of organization it is.
  • A neighborhood gem. A restaurant, mural, park, historic place or community space. Anyone can suggest one.

Pick the address

  1. Start typing the address or the place’s name.
  2. Pick it from the suggestions that appear.
  3. Check the pin on the small map below.

Not in the suggestions? Type it in anyway, and the Goldin team places the pin when they check it.

Send, then see it

After you send it, See it on the map takes you straight to your new place.

For the Goldin team

The admin page

Where you check new places and approve or reject them. Please check every few days: anything people add is visible until you review it.

Read the colors

  • Yellow: waiting for review. Shows on the map with a red outline.
  • Green: approved. Shows normally.
  • Red: rejected. Doesn’t show.

The page opens on Waiting for review, so new places come first. Use the tabs, Kind and Search to find anything else.

Review a place

  1. Click a yellow row to open it.
  2. Check it’s real, it’s in or near Chicago, and it’s a public place (never someone’s home).
  3. Look at the pin on the map. Wrong spot? Drag the pin or click where the place is, then Save the pin.
  4. Click Approve or Reject. The map updates at once.

You can’t edit a place’s details here. If something needs fixing, contact the person who manages the site.

Reject or Delete?

  • Reject takes it off the map but keeps it in the list. You can bring it back with Back to waiting.
  • Delete removes it for good. The page asks you to confirm. Use it only for spam, tests and duplicates.
Private details stay private

Each place has a section “Only for the Goldin team”: who sent it and how to reach them. It never appears on the public map. Use it only to contact people about their submission.
